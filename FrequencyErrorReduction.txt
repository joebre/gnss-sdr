Acquisition_1B.full_grid_search prevents the grid search process form exit the search at the first detected signal above threshold.
This avoids false acquisitions due to carrier frequency side lobes. This is necessary for a 100% loss of lock free run (side lobes are only 12 dB below the main lobe, for strong signals this becomes a source of "false" detection).

Correlation of Galileo E1-B signals over bit/secondary code boundary generates a power shift into the adjacent frequency regions. Considering that Galielo E1-B FLL uses a phase wrapped differential phase frequency discriminator which has a capture range of T/4, the above power shift into the nearby frequencies makes the loop unable to capture the signal which falls outside the FLL loop capture range. These cases are terminating in a carrier loss of lock situation (bit sync timeout or PLL not locking).
